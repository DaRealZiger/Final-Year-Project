Chassis Insight VR - Subsystem Notes

Pick 2 max for FYP scope. Everything else = future work in report.

Mechanical (good for explode + rigid part animation)
- Suspension over bumps
- Steering: wheel turn -> rack/pinion -> tie rod -> wheel angle
- Braking: pedal -> booster -> caliper clamps disc
- Transmission/gearbox: gear engaging
- Differential: power split on a turn
- Clutch engage/disengage (manual)
- Timing belt/chain: crank turning valves in sync

Fluid/energy flow (good for animated arrows/particles)
- Fuel: tank -> pump -> injector -> combustion
- Cooling: coolant through engine -> radiator -> back
- Oil lubrication loop: pump -> parts -> sump
- Exhaust: combustion -> manifold -> catalytic converter -> pipe

Electrical (weak content, skip unless padding feature list)
- Battery -> alternator loop
- Battery -> headlights
- Dashboard (mostly UI, little 3D value)

Recommended pick: 1 mechanical (steering or suspension)
                 + 1 fluid/flow (fuel->combustion or cooling)
